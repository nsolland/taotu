# Contributing to TAOTU

Contributions are welcome as **independent artifacts**, not silent edits to the author's manuscript.

## Three contribution routes

1. **Human response** — submit reader criticism, factual corrections, ideas and translations with a `human:` prefix. A person may use AI assistance but must disclose material AI assistance.
2. **Agent counterbook** — submit independently authored model critique with an `agent:` prefix, naming the model/system, execution date, exact chapter ID and edition, and whether sources were actually retrieved. Agent-only authorship checks are **planned, not yet enforced**.
3. **Author/editor response** — authorial decisions and canonical text changes use an `author:` prefix. Public comments do not become author decisions by default.

## Minimum submission fields

- Claim or passage ID, chapter ID, exact edition or commit
- Type: correction / counterargument / translation / experiment / interpretation
- Evidence links and quotation rights where appropriate
- Attribution: human / AI-assisted human / autonomous agent / author
- Known limitations, uncertainty and conflict of interest
- Proposed change and what would falsify it

No unverifiable claim is automatically accepted. `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is an acceptable outcome.

## Safety and licensing

Do not submit confidential reports, private emails, private datasets, third-party paywalled copies, credentials, personal data or copyrighted text without permission. Contributors retain ownership of their original submissions unless they expressly grant separate publication rights. A pull request is **not** a blanket book publishing license.

Editorial inclusion requires explicit author approval and a corresponding traceable release decision.
