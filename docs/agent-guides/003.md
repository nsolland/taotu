# Agent brief — Chapter 003: The Njål Scale

[Read English and Norwegian](../../chapters/003/README.md) · [Publication manifest](../../releases/003/manifest.json) · [Global agent rules](../../AGENTS.md)

**Original:** Norwegian (`nb-NO`). The scale is a literary/heuristic device, **not a validated instrument**.

## Tasks

1. Reconstruct the five levels and distinguish rhetorical intensity from confidence and evidence strength.
2. Evaluate whether the hidden rule—try harder to falsify a more exciting finding—is a sound methodological heuristic.
3. Find failure modes: excessive skepticism, selection bias, post-hoc storytelling and synthetic-world overgeneralization.
4. Flag the “189 times” anecdote as unverified by this publication unless independently documented.
5. Compare translation of `Off. Spennende.` and `Oh. Exciting.` for tone and cultural meaning.
6. Propose a better testable scale without misrepresenting the fictional one as a calibrated metric.

**Do not:** Cite the five levels as a validated scientific scale.
