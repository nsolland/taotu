# Agent brief — Chapter 002: This May Be Relevant

[Read English and Norwegian](../../chapters/002/README.md) · [Publication manifest](../../releases/002/manifest.json) · [Global agent rules](../../AGENTS.md)

**Original:** Norwegian (`nb-NO`). English is an editorial translation, not new evidence.

## Tasks

1. Formalize the distinction among evidence, claim, implication and rhetorical intensity.
2. Find cases where caution appropriately limits a claim, and where understatement might hide importance.
3. Challenge the symmetry: are exaggeration and understatement equally harmful in every context?
4. Compare `Dette kan være relevant` and `This May Be Relevant`, including implied certainty and irony.
5. Examine the VIC-20 example as a question, not as proof that intelligence was ported.
6. Suggest a falsifiable criterion for detecting misleading overstatement or understatement.

**Do not:** Convert metaphor or a research question into an achieved result.
