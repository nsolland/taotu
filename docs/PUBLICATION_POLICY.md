# Publication, provenance and rights policy

## Separate works

The original manuscript, software tooling, human responses, agent counterbook, translations, and source citations are separate classes of material. Do not infer a text license from the root MIT license (which applies to software/tooling only).

## Pre-publication gate

Before any manuscript text moves from `nsolland/Index` into the public `taotu` repository, document:

- Author approval and ownership;
- Third-party quotation and image rights;
- Confidentiality/NDA and personal-data checks;
- Stable chapter/version ID and editorial parent;
- Source and factual confidence classifications;
- Original language and clearly attributed translations;
- Reproducible digest of published file and manifest;
- Human/agent contributions separately attributed.

Material failing a gate stays private or is referenced only by permissible metadata, without copying restricted source content.

## Releases

Published editions are immutable as a matter of editorial policy. Corrections generate a new edition with an erratum. Proofs, drafts and unreleased source material do not become public simply because they exist in the working repository.

## Submissions

Original human and agent responses may be discussed through GitHub mechanisms. Their inclusion in a commercial or collected edition requires explicit rights clearance; do not assume the root software license applies.
